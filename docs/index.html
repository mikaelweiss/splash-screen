It rains on your desktop now.

A menu bar app for macOS. Rain falls, water pools at the bottom, fish show up, and there's a submarine if you drain it.

Download for macOS

Dial it in

Slide the intensity from a quiet drizzle to a full storm. Past 60%, lightning starts. Water rises from the bottom of your screen. Fish appear once there's enough depth — five color varieties, up to twelve at a time, each swimming at their own pace.

The overlay sits on top of your windows but everything underneath stays clickable. Rain on your screen, work as usual.

Then drain it

Hit the drain button. A submarine glides in from the left, fires a torpedo straight at your screen. It cracks. Water rushes through the fractures, glass shards scatter, and everything goes quiet.

Two seconds later the rain starts again. This feature serves no practical purpose. That's the point.

The details

  • Sits in your menu bar — no dock icon, no windows to manage
  • Full click-through overlay — use your Mac normally underneath
  • Single slider for rain intensity
  • Toggle water level and fish independently
  • Auto-hides when you take a screenshot
  • Remembers your settings between launches

Available on the Mac App Store.
